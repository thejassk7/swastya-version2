Problem Statement: Lack of awareness among parents regarding healthy eating of children.
By: K THEJAS SUVARNA

In India, major hospitals and cardiology networks report a 20% to 30% increase in heart related emergencies.
Data also highlights a worrying trend where up to 25% to 50% of recent cardiac events involve adults under the age of 40.
The Indian Council of Medical Reasearch evaluated concerns regarding sudden deaths among young Indians and concluded that lifestyle and eating habits as major reasons.
The main reason for all these is: Lack of awareness of parents regarding healthy eating of children.

According to report by National Family Health Survey, only 15% of toddlers in India receive a nutrionally adequate diet.
Core Areas of Awareness Defiency:
Many parents equate a full stomach with an adewquate diet. This results on low quality calories, biscuits and packaged commercial snacks to quiet hungry toddlers. Parents often miss essential micronutrients.
